Company knowledge

Back up deliberately.

Back up the whole data folder, including the keyring. Database files alone can restore as unreadable data. Stop the local engine before copying it unless using a supported backup procedure.

Provider credentials

Understand your setup.

Model calls use your configured credentials. The provider account and its billing remain your responsibility.

Alpha changes

Read each release.

The alpha is evolving. Follow the invite’s installation instructions and release notes for supported builds, migration steps and known limitations.
