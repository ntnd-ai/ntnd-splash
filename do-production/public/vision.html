The company we intend to build

From intention to an operating company.

Useful agents need more than a prompt. They need clear roles, the right context, permission to act and people who can direct the work.

01 / A company, expressed in work

More people should be able to turn an intention into a company.

The obstacle is often the distance between an idea and all the work it asks of you: research, communication, planning, making, learning.

AI agents can help close that distance. They become more useful when they have a role, relevant knowledge, a way to work and a clear relationship to your decisions. ntndOS brings those pieces into one company workspace.

02 / Knowledge that stays with the company

The next job should begin with more understanding.

Good work creates more than an output. It creates a better grasp of customers, a corrected assumption, a method worth using again.

We want that learning to become part of the company. Connecting delivered work, human corrections and reviewed knowledge is a central direction of the product. There is still work to do before the complete loop is dependable.

03 / Autonomy with a reason

Trust should have something underneath it.

An agent doing more is valuable when the work is useful and its authority is clear. A fast run that needs to be redone is not a victory. A correct result obtained outside its authority is not one either.

Our direction is to earn greater autonomy through evidence: useful results, understandable decisions, recoverable mistakes and permissions people knowingly grant.

04 / A world of possible companies

Small teams. Extraordinary range.

We are building for people with a strong intention and more work than hands. A researcher with a question. A founder with a customer to serve. A team ready to turn its knowledge into action.

Start with a single useful role. Grow the company from there.
