Good to know

Before you add.

Can I change an AI agent after I add it?

Yes. Edit its instructions, picture and description on its page, and set what it may do.

Which model does it use?

You choose: a local model through Ollama, a cloud provider with your own key, or OpenRouter. With OpenRouter you can keep to providers that keep none of your data.

What does it cost?

The AI agents are free, and so is the alpha. If you use a cloud model, your provider bills you directly.

What happens to my company knowledge?

Your Brain is kept on your Mac. With a local model, nothing leaves it. With a cloud model, the provider sees what an AI agent sends it, except pages you mark Local only, which go only if you say yes for that model.
