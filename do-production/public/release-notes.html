ntndOS Peridot · Alpha 0.1.1

17 November 2026

The first update to the ntndOS Peridot alpha adds semantic search, the Marketplace and OpenRouter, fixes how workflows change your files and makes screenshots work in Send feedback. If you're on 0.1.0, it arrives as an update in the app. New testers can apply through ntnd.ai/apply.

New

  • Semantic search. The Brain now finds pages by what they mean, not only by their exact words, so a question asked in your own words finds the right page more often. In English, searching for an exact name or wording works as well as before. This is a first version, and it gets better in coming updates.
  • Semantic search on the Brain screen. The search box on the Brain screen uses semantic search too.
  • Indexing after the update. After you update, your Brain is indexed once in the background. Until that's done, search matches words only, and the Brain screen says so.
  • The Marketplace. Pick a ready-made AI agent (Nova, Vera, Ava or Devon) and add it with "Add to your team". For now it has AI agents only. It's free.
  • A new company starts empty. A new company starts with no AI agents. Home shows "Add your first AI agent" with two ways in: "Add from the Marketplace" or "Create your own". Until you add one, the Brain map says "No map yet."
  • OpenRouter. Use models through OpenRouter with your own key. Add it in Settings › Model connections. As with other cloud AI, Local-only documents go to an OpenRouter model only if you say yes for that exact model; pictures never do.
  • Providers that keep none of your data. For OpenRouter, you can turn on "Only use providers that keep none of my data". The line under the switch reads: "When this is off, OpenRouter can use any company that runs the model, and some keep your data." Turning the switch off ends any Local-only yes you gave an OpenRouter model.
  • A line under your model. It says whether the model you chose has a provider that keeps none of your data. Once your key is added, ntndOS checks OpenRouter's list of those providers at most once a day, with the switch on or off.
  • Usage limits. If an AI provider you connected says your usage limit there is reached, the chat tells you: "Something went wrong: [provider] says your usage limit has been reached. Check your plan or limits with [provider], then try again."
  • New Brain documents from AI agents. An AI agent can propose a brand-new Brain document when nothing existing fits what it learned. Only that AI agent and you can read it.
  • "+ New document" on the Brain map. Start a new Brain document straight from the map.
  • Restore. A Brain document you archive now waits under "Archived" in Brain Files. Open it and click "Restore" to bring it back. It comes back unreviewed, and AI agents that can read it use it again.
  • Archiving asks about memories. When you archive a document, ntndOS asks whether the memories your AI agents made from it should be archived too, and tells you how many there are.
  • Local only asks about memories too. When you make a document Local only, ntndOS tells you how many memories your AI agents made from it that cloud AI can read. You choose "Make them Local only too" or "Keep them readable by cloud AI".
  • Agents, Memories and the decision log. The Agents and Memories pages, "Add to Brain" and the decision log are in this update.
  • Your own profile. Your page shows your role as Operator, and you can set your own name and title there.
  • Code in replies. Code in an AI agent's reply shows as code, in chats and rooms, with Copy and "Open in panel". Open in panel shows all the code on the right, with line numbers and which reply it came from. The chat gets narrower beside it.
  • Right-click. Right-click in a text box for Cut, Copy, Paste and Select All. Right-click selected text anywhere else to copy it.
  • "← Back" and "Reload" on the Community tab. Go back a page or reload the forum without leaving ntndOS. "← Back" turns on once there's a page to go back to. While you approve Send feedback on the forum, both buttons are hidden.
  • Chat has two tabs. "ai agents" holds your chats with AI agents, newest first. "people" holds your encrypted direct messages and rooms. "+ New chat" finds an AI agent by name or job and opens its chat.
  • Group chats with AI agents. In "+ New chat", tick two or more AI agents to start a group chat. Tag one with @ or "Mention", and only that AI agent answers. There are two exceptions for now: a tag on "Sam Lee" also tags an AI agent named "Sam", and an AI agent waiting for your answer takes your next message, whoever you tag. With no tag, everyone answers in turn, and each answer is its own reply with its own cost. You can pick another style under "Who answers" in the chat's settings.

Improved

  • Playbooks. Skills & packs now live in the Brain, as Playbooks. Open Brain › Files › Playbooks to read one, or write a new one with "+ New playbook" in the right panel. Settings no longer has a Skills & packs page.
  • One kind of workflow. There is now one kind of workflow. A workflow from before this update that was only text becomes a one-step workflow: one "Task for an AI agent" step with your text, word for word.
  • Approval cards. When an AI agent stops to ask you, one card in the chat shows what would change, with Approve and Reject side by side. The choice to always allow it is a separate tick under them, and it starts off.
  • Rooms ask once more. After this update, each room with an AI agent in it asks you once more to approve where that AI agent processes messages.
  • Unsaved changes. If you quit with changes you haven't saved, ntndOS asks first: "Unsaved changes", with Stay and Leave. If it can't finish tidying up as it quits, the app menu says "Cleanup paused, choose Quit again".
  • One forum connection. Send feedback and the Community tab now share one forum connection. Approve it once, and you're signed in on both. "Disconnect" in Settings › Account signs you out of the Community tab too.
  • Connect from Settings. "Connect" on the Send feedback row in Settings › Account now opens the forum in the Community tab, where you approve it. If the Community tab isn't showing the feedback forum, your browser opens instead. While you approve, the Community tab's bar has "Connect in your browser instead", in case the sign-in gets stuck.
  • Community tab loading. The Community tab says "Loading the forum…" while it loads. If it can't load, it says "The forum didn't load." with a "Try again" button.
  • See a file change before you approve it. When an AI agent asks to change a passage in a file, the approval card shows the passage before and after, line by line. Brain changes already worked this way. Switch between "inline" and "side by side".
  • When AI agents ask. One line now says when they ask: "Agents ask before using an action for the first time. Sending asks every time, except replies to you in Chat and rooms or tools you've allowed. Actions that cost money ask every time." It replaces the "Working mode" switch.
  • What an AI agent can do. What an AI agent can do comes from the tools and permissions you give it, not from its role name.
  • One Connectors page. Settings › Connectors replaces Settings › Apps, the Slack front desk page and Settings › Connected tools. Filter it by Mail & calendar, Files & notes, Chat, Research, Code or Marketing, or choose "Added by you" for tools you added.
  • Room notifications. In Settings › Notifications, "New room messages" uses the same switch as "Agent activity details".
  • "Note from ntndOS". Messages from ntndOS itself carry the label "Note from ntndOS".
  • Job roles. Chat shows each AI agent's job role.
  • Old conversations. A conversation with no current AI agent says "This history has no current AI agent. Start a new conversation to send a message."
  • Workflow updates. Messages from workflows now sit under "Workflow updates" at the bottom of Chat's "ai agents" tab.
  • New rooms ask who to invite. A room you just made asks who to invite first. Click "Later" to skip it.
  • Paused in Slack. While the AI agent at your Slack front desk is paused, newcomers get no reply. People you've already decided about hear "Sorry, I can't help — I'm paused."
  • Slack signature. It now reads "· ntnd AI agent ·".
  • Memories from archived documents. On the Memory tab, a memory hidden because its document was archived now names that document.
  • Folder marks. In Brain Files, a folder with the same name as a department is marked "(folder)".
  • A check before sharing. Sharing or unsharing a Brain document now asks you first.
  • Send feedback limits. If you send feedback too often, the form tells you when you can try again and keeps your message.
  • Audit record. Three more places keep what you type out of the audit record. Deleting a conversation now also removes the words kept beside the record for it, such as the text of its commands. The record keeps its lines, which can include what its AI agents searched for, any web addresses they opened, a coded reference to a draft's recipient, and its subject line.
  • Forum sign-in. Signing in to the forum from inside the app is ready for ntnd's own sign-in address.
  • Update helper. It now runs on a newer Node.js security release.
  • Words. More screens say "AI agent", and most screens now use US spelling.
  • Light mode. The light-mode sidebar uses the same plum as dark mode.

Removed

  • Cloud spending. Cloud spending is gone: the Cloud spending page, the spending limit in Setup, and the spend figures. Each AI provider's own site shows what you spend.

Fixed

  • Tags in rooms. In a room, a message that tags someone now goes out. Only the people you tagged are alerted, and everyone in the room can still read it.
  • The right room. A room message always goes to the room you have open.
  • Workflow edits. A workflow step that changes one passage in a file now changes just that passage. If the passage isn't in the file exactly once, the step or its card says so and nothing changes. If you used one on 0.1.0, see Updates.
  • Local-only material in workflows. When a workflow step's work holds Local-only material, ntndOS saves a Local-only copy and leaves your file as it was. A new file meant for a synced folder is saved in Local only instead. You'll find copies in the Local only list in Govern › Permissions. A new file kept in Local only doesn't show in that list yet.
  • Workflows follow the Brain search switch. A workflow step no longer reads your Brain when its AI agent has Search Company Brain turned off. A step that searches the Brain stops and says why, and an AI step runs without your Brain and says so.
  • Steps that stop partway. If a step that changes files one item at a time stops partway, it now says which item stopped it and that the items before it were done. It used to say nothing changed.
  • Workflow task steps. A step that makes several tasks now checks every task's title and project before it makes the first one. If a title is missing or a project can't be found, it makes no tasks and says what to fix.
  • File cards no longer promise undo. Approval cards for writing or changing a file, and for saving a draft, now say the change can't be undone, because ntndOS doesn't keep the earlier version. Before, they said it could be.
  • Add a screenshot. In Send feedback, "Add a screenshot" works in the desktop app. ntndOS takes the picture of its own window, so macOS doesn't ask for screen recording. You see the picture before anything is sent, and you can black out parts of it with "Black-out marker".
  • "Brain search is off". When Search Company Brain is off for an AI agent, its reply now carries a quiet "Brain search is off" line. It used to say the company brain was unreachable. New AI agents start with their tools off. To let one use your Brain, turn on Search Company Brain on its Tools tab.
  • Attach and Send in a new room. After you create a room in Chat and invite someone or click "Later", Attach and Send work straight away. If the chat server is still busy, ntndOS says so and keeps your words. Attach and Send also come back after a file send goes through.
  • Pause and Resume. Pause stops an AI agent everywhere, and Resume undoes only a pause made with Pause. The AI agent's page shows the change straight away.
  • Names on the Memory page. The Memory page shows each AI agent's current name. An AI agent you let go keeps its name, marked "let go".
  • Setting up. ntndOS starts, and setup finishes, on Macs without Apple's developer tools.
  • Community tab. The Community tab connects to the forum out of the box.
  • Deleting memories. If "Delete them" or "Delete them too" deletes nothing, ntndOS now says "Nothing was deleted. Try again, or delete the memories one by one in each agent's Memory tab."
  • People panel. The People panel no longer shows "Could not read current membership" for someone who joined by invite.
  • Workflow switches. Each branch of a switch is now labeled with when it's taken, including "when nothing above matched".
  • Skills. A skill whose instructions aren't on this Mac says so: "This skill's instructions aren't on this Mac yet. You can remove it."
  • Duplicate agent. After "Duplicate agent", the copy shows straight away.
  • Govern. Govern no longer links to a guide you can't open.
  • Memory limits. AI agent tools keep to their memory limit in two more cases.

Updates

If you're on 0.1.0, you get 0.1.1 as an update in the app. Your company, chats and files stay.

To update now, choose Settings › Check for ntndOS updates… in the menu bar, then "Install and restart".

If an AI agent is still working, nothing is installed, and ntndOS names the AI agent. Let it finish or stop it, then try again. If the update can't finish, ntndOS says "You're still on Alpha 0.1.0, and nothing changed." and shows a support code.

If you installed ntndOS for everyone on this Mac and the update can't install, ask us on the forum before you reinstall.

Check your files. On 0.1.0, a workflow step that changes one passage replaced the whole file with nothing when you said yes. If you used one, check that file. 0.1.1 fixes this.

On 0.1.0, a workflow step that wrote a file after a read step could replace that file with a fixed line, or an AI step's reply to that line, instead of what was read. If you used one, check that file too.

When the next update is ready, an "Update" tag shows beside the version in the sidebar. Click it to install.

Your version

The sidebar shows "ntndOS Peridot" with "Alpha 0.1.1" under it. Settings › About shows "Version 0.1.1 (Alpha)", and "Copy details" copies it with the build number.

Privacy in this build

The Community tab shows our forum, community.ntnd.ai, by itself. Once you've opened the tab, ntndOS asks the forum for its name when it starts, when you open the tab, or when you change connected tools (at most every 10 minutes). It sends nothing about you; the forum sees your internet address, like any website.

  • Reliability sharing (optional) sends a small signal with the app version when ntndOS starts, a daily ping to see if it is on, and one when you uninstall. Never your chats, Brain, files or prompts. This helps us understand which versions of the app are still active and how much the app is used.
  • License and update checks are separate. ntndOS needs them to work, so they are always on.

Known issues

  • Search in Arabic and Russian. In Arabic and Russian, search can find the right page less often than in 0.1.0. We're working on a fix.
  • Semantic search always shows something. Semantic search shows the closest pages it finds, even when nothing in your Brain answers the question. Check that a page really answers before you rely on it.
  • Read steps. A workflow step can't yet use what a "read file", "list dir" or "run command" step handed on. The workflow stops there and says so. To work with what was read, give the whole job to one AI agent in a single step.
  • Armed Slack channels. Arming a Slack channel does not let an AI agent post there in 0.1.1. Each armed post is stopped and says so; leave channels unarmed so each post asks you.
  • File changes can't be undone. File write, edit and draft cards carry the "irreversible" mark. In Govern › Rules & controls › Available actions, writing and editing files, and saving drafts, show as "medium risk". Keep your own backups.
  • Local-model output. An AI step that runs on a local model and draws on Local-only Brain material leaves that material in its own output file, unmarked.
  • Proposed AI agents. In a workflow, a "propose hire" step drops the skills it lists.
  • Failed commands. In a workflow, a command step you allowed can show as done when the command failed. Check the run's result.
  • Community tab buttons. "← Back" and "Reload" can stay hidden for 10 minutes or more while a connection waits. "← Back" can also stay gray after you connect.
  • Uploads in Chat. An upload made while the chat server is busy can end with "Upload outcome unknown".
  • Workflows paused during the update. A workflow paused before the update on a card you had already approved may say a step did not run when it did. Check its result before running it again.
  • A box named "previous". After the update, the first time ntndOS asks to use speech recognition, macOS can name the app "previous" instead of ntndOS. It is ntndOS asking. The old 0.1.0 copy also stays on your Mac until you uninstall ntndOS. We're working on a fix.
  • Names on approval cards. A card's who-sees-it line can include a folder or file name an AI agent chose, shown inside ntndOS's own wording.

Limits carried over from 0.1.0:

  • Mac with Apple silicon only, for now.
  • /hire places the new AI agent in a built-in starter department you name. Your own departments, and a starter department that was renamed or already holds documents, need placing by hand.
  • Hiding can't pull back what was already sent by Slack, Matrix or email.
  • A few places can still show what came from a document you archived, made Local only, stopped sharing or moved to the Trash. Those places include an AI agent's own shell commands, workflow results, chat transcripts, a file renamed in Finder, and the activity record if an AI agent used those words in a search or a web address.
  • If voice input doesn't work right after you install, quit ntndOS and open it again.

This is an experimental alpha. Use sample data, keep your own backups and review what you let agents do.

AlphamacOS (Apple silicon)

ntndOS Peridot · Alpha 0.1.0

25 October 2026

The first public version of ntndOS Peridot is here, as an invite-only alpha for the Mac. Apply for a license through ntnd.ai/apply

What ntndOS is

ntndOS is an agentic harness that runs on your Mac. You set up AI agents, give them work and decide what happens next. Keep your data local, and choose a local model or your own cloud-model key.

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon.
  • macOS 13.5 or later.
  • A license code from the alpha. The alpha is free. You only pay for any cloud LLM provider you may be using, or you can run it for free with local LLMs.

Get access

  1. Request a place at ntnd.ai/apply.
  2. Once you're approved, we email you. Sign in at license.ntnd.ai/account with that address for your license code and the download.
  3. Each license works on one Mac, and the email says when it ends. Moving to another Mac? Use Replace your Mac on the same page. If you ever need help or would like an extra slot, just contact us.

Install and set up

  1. Install. Open Install ntndOS from the download and choose Install. You can find your download and license key once approved also on license.ntnd.ai/account.
  2. Activate. Enter your license code. It's checked online and isn't saved on this Mac. ntndOS checks again from time to time and works offline for up to 30 days.
  3. Set up on one screen. Give your company a name. Keep the suggested ntnd folder (~/ntnd) or choose another; iCloud Drive and Dropbox work too. Your agents keep their files there, and tools on your Mac can work only inside it. Agree to the alpha Terms, decide on reliability sharing, then choose Confirm and continue.

What's in v0.1.0

Agents. Add AI agents, group them into departments or plan a team. Each agent can Propose first or Act within permissions.

Chat. Talk to your agents, or dictate; speech is transcribed on your Mac. Connect chat to message people in end-to-end encrypted rooms. Keep your chat recovery key (Settings › Chat) safe. A new Mac needs it. To delete a conversation, open its settings; you choose whether its notes and files stay.

Home, Work and approvals. Home shows what needs your decision, what your agents are doing and what came back today. When an agent wants to act outside the chat, it asks first, unless you've already allowed that action. Each approval card shows one exact action, which you approve or deny. Work has four tabs: Approvals, Tasks, Delivered and Requests.

Calendar and Workflows. An event an agent proposes waits for your OK. Workflows are chains of steps on a canvas; run one now and its history stays.

Brain. The Brain holds what your company knows. Files lists it, and Map shows how it connects. Connect a folder on your Mac, a GitHub repository, Google Drive or Notion. The Brain keeps its copy up to date, and you keep editing the original. Mark a document Local only, and AI in the cloud can't read it unless you approve that on a card.

Notes follow their sources. Every note an agent makes remembers which documents it came from. Close a document, make it Local only or move it to the Trash, and what agents made from it is hidden. ntndOS asks once: hide, keep or delete. Before anything is deleted, you see exactly which items, and you type delete to confirm.

Govern. Decide what your agents may do on their own. For each tool, pick Ask every time, Draft first or, where offered, Always. Activity is the verified record of every decision.

Models.

  • On your Mac: in the menu bar, choose Settings › Local models… to connect your own Ollama server (http://127.0.0.1:11434 by default). LM Studio works through the OpenAI-compatible endpoint in Settings › Model connections.
  • In the cloud, with your own key: Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok and Hugging Face. Keys are stored on your Mac and used only to call that provider, which bills you directly. Settings › Cloud spending shows this month's cost against your ceiling.

Connections. Settings › Apps covers Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, GitHub, Slack, Obsidian and web search; each says how far it reaches. Settings › Connected tools adds tools for your agents through MCP. Adding one gives nobody access, and the first call to any tool stops for you.

Updates

  • Already testing the alpha on build 100? You don't need to download anything: ntndOS offers v0.1.0 as an update, and your company, chats and files stay.
  • ntndOS checks for updates every 6 hours and soon after your Mac wakes. To check now, choose Settings › Check for ntndOS updates… in the menu bar. With nothing new, it says "ntndOS is up to date."
  • When one is ready, an Update label appears in the sidebar. Choose Install and restart. ntndOS finishes pending work, then restarts.
  • From v0.1.0 on: if an agent is still working, nothing is installed, and ntndOS names the agent. Let it finish or stop it, then try again.
  • From v0.1.0 on: if an update can't finish, you'll see "You're still on Alpha 0.1.0, and nothing changed." with a support code. Your work is untouched.

Your version

The sidebar shows ntndOS Peridot, Alpha 0.1.0. Settings › About shows Version 0.1.0 (Alpha). For support, Copy details adds the build number.

Uninstall

In the menu bar, choose Settings › Uninstall ntndOS… The app closes and moves to the Trash. Your chats, workspace and settings stay unless you tick "Also delete my ntndOS data"; once ticked, that can't be undone. Ticking it also offers a second box to move your ntnd folder to the Trash (not offered for a folder in iCloud Drive). If ntndOS can reach our license server, uninstalling also frees this Mac's place on your license. If not, use Replace your Mac.

Privacy in this build

  • Reliability sharing (optional) sends a small signal with the app version when ntndOS starts, a daily ping to see if it is on, and one when you uninstall. Never your chats, Brain, files or prompts. This helps us understand which versions of the app are still active and how much the app is used.
  • License and update checks are separate. ntndOS needs them to work, so they are always on.

Known limits

  • Mac with Apple silicon only, for now.
  • The Marketplace is a preview on the Coming soon page. It doesn't work yet.
  • An agent's /hire places the new agent in a built-in starter department you name. Your own departments, and a starter department that was renamed or already holds documents, need placing by hand.
  • Hiding can't pull back what an agent already sent by Slack, Matrix or email.
  • A few places can still show what came from a closed document: an agent's own shell commands, workflow results, chat transcripts and a file renamed in Finder.

This is an experimental alpha. Use sample data, keep your own backups and review what you let agents do.
