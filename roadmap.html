The current app

Our first release
comes first.

Create agents, connect them to your company context and build workflows in one workspace on your machine. The current ntndOS app is the foundation—not a promise that everything on this roadmap is included.

The first alpha release is for macOS; Linux and Windows follow. Packaging and release testing are a separate workstream from the V2 proposals below.

Beyond the first release

A sequence built
around useful work.

Small and medium businesses first. Each step needs evidence before the next one expands the scope. These are proposed directions; some ingredients already exist in the current app.

01
V2 · usability proposal

Make everyday work
easier to navigate.

A clearer view of what needs you, what is progressing, and what is ready to review—without losing the controls that make the workspace useful.

  • A readable, resizable workflow inspector with room for real procedures.
  • Explore a dedicated Approvals view inside Work; keep Govern for rules, permissions and oversight.
  • Preserve the original Brain, Files views, spatial workflow graphs and Calendar.
What would make this a useful improvement?

Existing controls and links still work. Edits persist. Keyboard and smaller-screen use remain practical. Moving an approval never changes its meaning or creates a second decision record. The final navigation remains subject to product review.

02
V2 · core work cycle

Carry a job from
intent to accepted result.

Connect the project, agent, knowledge, workflow, human contribution and external action into one understandable chain of work.

  • Human tasks, questions and reviews with an owner, a reason and a response route.
  • Exact decisions, bounded runs, meaningful pause and stop behavior, and recoverable progress.
  • Evidence of what happened, followed by a separate check that the result meets the brief.
  • Calendar proposals that become commitments only when accepted; deadlines are not automatic bookings.
An illustrative job

Prepare a course launch → ask a teacher to review → confirm the offer → review an invitation → send through the agreed integration → verify the result. A fictional studio is the test scenario, not a limitation on the product.

What needs to be demonstrated?

One whole path works in the intended production runtime, including interruption, denial, changed inputs, expiry, revoked access and uncertain external effects. The person supervising it can explain what is waiting, what is allowed and what actually happened. Existing ingredients must be checked before new implementation is added.

03
V2 · first-party Library

Start with a method
you can inspect.

NTND-authored skills, workflow templates and starter roles—so a company can build on useful methods without inheriting someone else’s private workspace.

  • See the purpose, publisher, compatible version, connections and requested permissions.
  • Preview an example, try sample data and create an independent company instance.
  • Review version updates and any expanded access before applying them.
What is the boundary?

A reusable template is not an installed employee. Credentials, private memory and standing permissions do not transfer with the method. The first milestone is a working install and update path for one skill, one workflow and one starter role—not an open third-party marketplace.

04
Later · commercial pilots

Acquire capabilities.
Offer what you have built.

Explore a market where companies can install a reviewed role or hire a seller-operated AI service. Both models remain open; neither is selected for launch.

Install a role

Bring a versioned method into your company, then configure a fresh identity, model, connections and access.

Hire a service

Agree a deliverable, bounded inputs, price, deadline and acceptance criteria. The seller operates the service.

  • Support both buyers and sellers: trials, quotes, capacity, delivery, revisions and resolution.
  • Explore structured hiring and payment cards in Chat that open the same engagement record.
  • Prepare a separate commercial release from reviewed reusable knowledge, not raw company memory.
What remains undecided?

The first commercial model, primary hiring entry point, payment provider, seller terms and release-review process. Live commerce needs tested access boundaries, payment controls and failure handling. Open publishing, exclusive transfers and decentralized settlement are separate later choices.

05
Later · deployment-led expansion

Grow the responsibility
with the evidence.

Enterprise administration and carefully calibrated autonomy should answer real deployment needs—not make every small company operate like a large one.

  • Explore organizational identity, administrative roles, multi-stage review and evidence export.
  • Add retention, residency and incident operations when a deployment requires them.
  • Evaluate wider autonomy at a narrow task scope, with uncertainty, review and rollback rules.
What does “earned” mean here?

Observed accepted outcomes may support a reviewable policy change. A confidence or trust score must not override a private boundary or silently grant new authority. The measure is better work with proportionate supervision, not a longer unattended run.

06
Later · research direction

Let agents work
across companies.

Explore how an agent in one NTND company could request help from an agent in another, with a specific purpose and boundaries both owners can inspect.

  • Define each agent’s identity and the exact room, task, data and capability scope.
  • Require deliberate authorization on both sides; a human passport alone does not authorize an agent.
  • Prevent reply loops and unintended handoffs, and keep a reviewable record of what was exchanged and done.
What works now, and what does not?

In the closed-alpha design, a person on one NTND installation can be approved to use another company’s agent in a shared room. Agent-originated requests to another company’s agent are currently denied. Cross-company agent-to-agent communication is a future exploration, not an alpha capability or a committed release date.

What keeps the sequence honest

Evidence before
the next promise.

Research informs these choices; it does not prove demand for NTND. The app, prototype, proposed design and released behavior remain distinct.

One record, wherever you meet it.

Work, Chat, Calendar and the Brain should point to the same job and decisions. A new screen must not become a competing source of truth.

Less supervision, not just more activity.

Track accepted outcomes, corrections, review time, blocked work and cost per accepted result. Prototype test counts are not customer traction.

Dates follow validated scope.

Ordering reflects dependencies. Release notes and platform guidance will describe the actual build. This page is not a delivery-date commitment.

Bring a job worth doing.

Tell us what you want your company’s agents to help with. Keep confidential information out of your access request; a clear description of the work is enough.
