A conversation with boundaries

Not another place
for messages to disappear.

A useful company conversation carries more than words. It needs a clear membership, a way to establish trust, and a deliberate answer to whether an agent may take part.

01

People belong
in the room.

Invite the colleagues needed for the work. Room membership is visible and does not silently open the rest of the company.

02

Know who is
asking.

Matrix verification checks a device. A private passport lets an agent’s company review a person’s standing and how long it lasts. Neither grants agent access on its own.

03

An agent joins
by decision.

The agent’s owner reviews the exact person, room and agent before allowing a reply. Room membership and a passport are not blanket permission.
