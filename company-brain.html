A shared place to inspect what shaped the work

More than retrieval

Context that keeps
its shape.

Useful company knowledge is not just a collection of files. It includes who and what it relates to, where it belongs, which decisions shaped it, and which view makes sense for the question at hand.

Perspective

Orient before you search.

Start from people, functions, knowledge, governance, commercial work, estate or the wider world—not an undifferentiated document pile.

Relationships

Keep a thread to the material.

A map can make the relationship around a document legible: its dimension, connected work and the other ideas that give it meaning.

Judgment

Make the next question better.

Use the company’s evolving context to decide where to look next, while keeping human judgment and permission separate from retrieval.

Where it starts to matter

A shared frame
for useful work.

Company Brain is not an ornamental layer over the product. It is a way to make company context inspectable when people and agents need to act with care.

A new agent

Give a role a better starting point.

Connect a role to the methods, material and company areas that are relevant to its job—without treating everything in the company as universally available context.
