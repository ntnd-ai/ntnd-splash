Scroll to see the whole picture.

7 dimensions

Agent profiles, roles, teams and responsibilities. The people and agents behind the work, and how they relate to one another.Examples: Research assistant, Content writer, Support team.

Tasks, workflows and working methods. How your company gathers feedback, improves its work and carries out recurring jobs.Examples: Review customer feedback, Revise a draft, Prepare a weekly report.

Documents, research, facts and reference material. The evidence and context that help agents produce work grounded in your company.Examples: Brand voice guide, Product handbook, Meeting notes.

Objectives, policies, rules and decision records. What the company is trying to do, and the boundaries that guide its work.Examples: Approval to publish, Customer data policy, Quarterly goals.

Customer and partner context, offers and commercial plans. The knowledge behind how your company creates and exchanges value.Examples: Customer brief, Service proposal, Pricing plan.

Software, tools, services and infrastructure records. The systems your company relies on, and how those systems fit together.Examples: Company website, Shared file storage, Email service.

Market research, external sources and industry developments. What is changing outside your company, and what you can learn from it.Examples: Industry news, Competitor announcement, Market trends.
