Signup privacy notice

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Operated by ntnd.ai. For questions about your information or access request, use our contact form.

This notice covers the ntndOS alpha signup, which requests early access and subscribes you to ntnd product updates. The app and community services have separate privacy information.

What we collect and why

When you sign up, we collect your name, email, platform preference, main interest and a short description of what you would like to try. Company or project details are optional. Required fields are marked on the form; we need those details to process your request.

We use those details to verify your email, review your application, organise testing groups and contact you about access. We also use your email to send ntnd product updates. Invitations are reviewed by a person, not decided automatically.

We also keep a record of your signup and confirmation, application status, email preferences and relevant correspondence. Technical records, including your IP address and email-delivery information, help us prevent abuse and resolve problems. Please do not include passwords, API keys or confidential or sensitive information in your application.

Emails and your choices

One signup requests alpha access and subscribes you to ntnd product updates, including alpha announcements, new features and releases. The form makes both purposes clear before you submit. One email confirmation confirms both; there is no separate subscription step. We do not send product updates before you confirm.

Product updates continue beyond the alpha until you unsubscribe. Every product-update email includes an unsubscribe link; you do not need to email us or sign in. You can also use the contact form for help. We email you about your request, including verification, confirmation and any invitation. These messages do not grant access themselves.

Our legal basis

We rely on legitimate interests to manage the requested access programme, answer questions and protect the service. Those interests are organising a manageable test, communicating with applicants and preventing misuse. You can object to this processing or withdraw your request.

For product-update emails, we ask for your consent through the clearly labelled signup and email confirmation. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Where processing is required by law, we rely on that legal obligation.

Who we share information with

Information is available to people administering the access programme and to service providers that support website/application hosting, email delivery and support correspondence. They receive the information needed for those services. We may also disclose information where legally required.

We do not sell your information, share it for other organisations' marketing or use your application to train AI models.

Some service providers process information outside the European Economic Area. Where required, transfers must be covered by an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards, such as approved contractual clauses. Use our contact form to ask about the safeguards applying to your data or request a copy.

How long we keep it

  • Unconfirmed signups: deleted after 7 days from submission.
  • Alpha list: confirmed applications are kept for up to 18 months from email verification, or deleted sooner when no longer needed.
  • Product-update subscriptions: your email address and subscription record are kept until you unsubscribe, or until we stop providing those updates. They are not subject to the alpha list's 18-month limit.
  • Routine technical and delivery records: kept for up to 30 days, unless needed for a specific security incident or legal matter.
  • Support correspondence: kept for up to 18 months after the matter is resolved.

Deleted information may remain in restricted backups for up to 30 days before those copies expire. We may retain limited records where necessary to meet a legal obligation, handle a dispute or honour an unsubscribe. These are not used to resume marketing. Joining the alpha does not automatically extend the waiting list's retention period.

When we delete an expired alpha application, we do not keep its company details, platform preference or task description just because you still receive product updates. We retain only the information needed for the continuing subscription.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, restrict its use, object to processing, or receive a portable copy where applicable. Use our contact form; we may need to verify that the request is yours. Where GDPR applies, we normally respond within one month and explain any permitted extension.

You can unsubscribe from product updates anytime. Unsubscribing does not itself withdraw your access request. If you ask us to delete all your signup information, we remove your application and subscription, subject to the limited exceptions above.

You can complain to a competent data-protection authority, including the authority where you live or work in the EEA, without contacting us first.

Tracking and changes

This limited signup flow does not use optional advertising analytics or email-open and click tracking. Necessary browser preferences and security records are separate.

If we materially change this notice or who is responsible for the service, we will explain the change. Existing opt-outs and deletion dates carry forward; a change does not give us permission to use your information for unrelated purposes.
